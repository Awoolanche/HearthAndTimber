**Fixed**
* Swamp Cypress Shingles recipe failing to load due to a misspelled Blooming Nature slab id
* Double Shingle Slabs dropping only one slab
* Pale Oak Board dropping nothing when broken
* Waxed Thatch being unobtainable, Honeycomb now waxes Thatch, Thatch Stairs and Thatch Slabs
* Waxed Thatch names not being translated
* Wheat and Hoe on Thatch Stairs and Slabs turning them into full blocks or doing nothing

* Thatch, Terracotta Shingle and Patchwork Shingle Stairs and Slabs as well as Timber Frame Stairs to the stairs and slabs tags
